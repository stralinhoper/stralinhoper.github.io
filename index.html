BUILT

Sustainable Aquatic/Wetland Biodome

A team engineering project (with Mohamed Abul Bashar, Ajani Sahur Inyang, and Elijah Oglesby): a working freshwater wetland/aquatic ecosystem model. Built the tank from 1/4" acrylic sheets solvent-welded and silicone-sealed, reinforced with an aluminum extrusion frame, then added gravel/sand filtration, pumps, and live minnows as water-quality indicators. A micro:bit and Hummingbird Bit handled monitoring — a light sensor pair tracked filtration performance and a distance sensor read water level without touching the water. The tank held water with zero leaks and the water cleared up over testing. The original plan (a second, smaller tank nested inside the first) didn't fit once the metal frames were built — next iteration calls for slimmer frames from the start.
